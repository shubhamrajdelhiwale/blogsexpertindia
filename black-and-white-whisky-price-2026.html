By Blogs Expert India • August 26, 2026 • 18 min read

The Black and White whisky price remains one of the most searched topics among Scotch whisky buyers in India. The reason is simple: Black & White is one of the country's most familiar blended Scotch whisky brands, but its retail price can change significantly depending on where the bottle is purchased.

A buyer in Delhi may find a different price from someone purchasing the same 750ml bottle in Mumbai. The rate in Goa can also differ from Bangalore, Hyderabad, Punjab, Kerala or Kolkata. This is mainly because alcohol pricing in India is influenced by state-level excise duties, taxes, distribution structures and local regulations.

That is why searching for a single nationwide price can sometimes be confusing.

Black & White has a long history and remains part of the portfolio of Diageo India. The company describes the brand as a smooth and balanced Scotch associated with James Buchanan's ambition to create a whisky for a broad audience. The brand is also instantly recognisable because of its famous Scottie and Westie terriers.

For 2026, the best way to understand the Black and White whisky price in India is to compare bottle sizes, check state-level price trends and verify the latest MRP before purchasing.

This detailed guide covers the 180ml, 375ml and 750ml bottles, estimated state-wise prices, flavour profile, history, brand comparisons and buying information.

Black & White is a Blended Scotch Whisky with a long connection to James Buchanan.

Diageo India says James Buchanan created Black & White in 1904 with the mission of making a whisky for everyone. The company describes the brand as smooth, balanced and designed for sharing.

The brand is part of Diageo India's wider portfolio, which includes several major whisky brands.

Black & White is particularly famous for its distinctive black-and-white label and two terrier characters.

The history of Black & White is more interesting than many buyers realise.

Diageo India's brand information connects the brand with Buchanan's Blend, which was created in 1879 and became distinguished by its black-and-white label. The nickname eventually developed into the official brand name.

The modern Black & White brand story is also linked to James Buchanan's mission to create an accessible whisky, with Diageo India dating Black & White to 1904.

This combination of historical references explains why both dates are often connected with the brand:

  • 1879: Buchanan's Blend and the earlier black-and-white identity
  • 1904: Black & White as the established brand identity highlighted by Diageo India

The two terriers are one of the strongest visual elements of the brand.

They are known as:

  • Scottie – the black Scottish Terrier
  • Westie – the white West Highland White Terrier

According to Diageo India, James Buchanan visited a dog show and was inspired by the champion terriers he saw there. The contrasting dogs later became the famous symbols of Black & White.
